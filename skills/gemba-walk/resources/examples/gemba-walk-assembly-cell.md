## Pre-Walk Plan — Assembly Cell 3

**Route:** Station 1 (housing prep) → Station 2 (bearing press) → Station 3 (seal install) → Station 4 (shaft assembly) → Station 5 (final torque and pack) — process flow order, not the layout's physical order.

**Open questions per lens (3–5 each):**

*Safety:* Any pinch points at the bearing press? Anything stored above shoulder height? Clear path to the exit at Station 4?

*Quality:* Where does rework happen if a housing is misaligned? Is there a sorting or inspection step that isn't on the process sheet? What changed recently that isn't reflected in standard work?

*Flow:* Where does WIP build up between stations? Is Station 2 running ahead of Station 3? Any batching happening that isn't designed in?

*Standard Work:* Are operators following the posted sequence at each station? Is the standard work document current, or has it been hand-modified?

*Engagement:* Does the team know their first-pass yield today? Is the cell's board updated this shift?
