**User:** What should the supervisor actually look for when they do walk it, so they're not just eyeballing it?

**Assistant:** A few concrete things, so two people looking at the same crib would score it the same way:

- **For Sort:** Pull every item off the shelves and shadow boards and sort it into "used in the last 30 days" versus "not used." Anything in the second pile gets a red tag and a disposition decision — return to stock elsewhere, or remove. A crib scores a 2 only if that tagging is already happening as a routine practice, not just once for this audit.
- **For the specialty gauges specifically:** Count how many of the labeled gauge locations are currently empty at the moment of the walk, not "how often they're usually full." An empty labeled location is a Set in Order defect regardless of why the gauge is missing.
- **For Shine on the tools themselves:** Pick three tools at random and check them for wear, corrosion, or damage that would go unnoticed under a wipe-down alone. If none of the three show any inspection-level attention, Shine stays at 1 even if the counter looks spotless.
