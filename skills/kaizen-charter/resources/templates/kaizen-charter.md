# Kaizen Charter Template

## 1. Business case
[The number tied to a real consequence — cost, safety, delivery, quality, capacity — and the decision it supports. Every number carries a source tag.]

## 2. Scope
[Exact start point (trigger) and end point (handoff) of the process this event covers.]

## 3. Boundaries and constraints
[What can't change during the week — equipment availability, staffing, budget, systems that can't be touched. Specific enough to test against a real proposal.]

## 3a. Readiness assumptions and risks
[Has the process been observed recently? Is it stable? Is the team truly freed for the week? Is the problem understood well enough to scope? Named plainly, not silently assumed.]

## 8. Agenda
### 5-day variant
| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Current-state mapping, baseline confirmation |
| 2 | Root cause / waste identification |
| 3 | Future-state design, countermeasure selection |
| 4 | Implement, test, adjust |
| 5 | Stabilize, document standard work, report-out |

### 3-day variant (use when current-state map and data are already in hand)
| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm current state, design future state |
| 2 | Implement and test |
| 3 | Stabilize, document, report-out |

## 9. Deliverables
[What exists at the end of the week — a new layout, a standard work sheet, a countermeasure list with owners, an updated process map.]

## 10. Report-out plan
[Who presents, to whom, what's covered — before/after metrics, what was tried, what remains open.]
