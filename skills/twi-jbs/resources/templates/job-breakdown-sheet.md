## JI 4-step training plan

1. **Prepare the worker** — put at ease, state the job, find out what they already know, get them interested.
2. **Present the operation** — tell, show, illustrate one Important Step at a time; stress Key Points; go slowly, clearly, patiently, no more than they can master.
3. **Try out performance** — have them do the job; have them explain Key Points as they go; correct errors; continue until you know they know.
4. **Follow up** — put them on their own; designate who to go to for help; check frequently; taper off extra coaching.
